## Read this first

Grace answers your phone. She tells callers your hours, prices and policies, books appointments, and
puts people through to your team when she should.

**She can only ever say things you have written down here.** She does not guess, she does not
improvise, and she does not look things up on the internet. If a caller asks something you have not
answered in this form, she says *"let me have the front desk confirm that for you"* and takes a
message. That is the safety net — but every blank in this form is a caller who gets that answer
instead of a booking.

So this form is, quite literally, **everything Grace is allowed to say to your customers.**

### How to fill it in

**Write real sentences, not one-word answers.** You will notice there are almost no tick boxes in
this form. That is deliberate. A tick box lets us guess what you meant; your own words tell us what
you actually do. Where we have written *"Make sure your answer covers"*, please work through each
point — those are the details that come up on real phone calls.

**Answer as if you were training a new receptionist on her first day.** Someone capable, but who
knows nothing about massage or about how you work. Everything obvious to you is invisible to her.

**Write the way you would speak.** Grace reads your answers aloud, so "we're open till eight thirty
every night" works better than "Operating hours: 2030".

**Nothing is too small.** The questions that get asked most on the phone are the small ones —
parking, tipping, what to wear, how early to arrive.

**Do not guess.** If you are unsure, write *"ask me"* and move on. A blank we know about is safe. A
wrong answer gets said out loud to a real customer.

**You do not have to do this in one sitting.** Each section shows roughly how long it takes. Section
1 is the one that unblocks everything else — please do that one first, today if you can.

⭐ = we cannot go live without it.
